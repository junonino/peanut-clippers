## A David vs. Goliath Matchup

Comparing the Wahl Peanut to the BabylissPro FX series (like the FX707 or Skeleton FX) is like comparing a reliable sedan to a luxury sports car. Both get you to the destination, but the experience and the cost are vastly different.

---

## The Biggest Difference: The Blade

The **BabylissPro FX** is famous for its exposed "360-degree" T-blade. This allows for zero-gap adjustment and incredible visibility while lining up a client. You can see exactly where the blade hits the skin without the clipper body getting in the way.

The **Wahl Peanut** has a standard blade assembly. It's great for detail work, but it lacks the "open window" view that the Babyliss provides.

---

## Build Quality and Weight

The **Peanut** is a lightweight plastic tool. It's durable, but it feels like a tool. 

The **Babyliss FX** is usually all-metal or has a very high-end finish. It has "weight" and heft, which many professional barbers prefer for stability during intricate lineups.

---

## Price and Value

The **Peanut** wins the value war every time. At ~$30, you can buy three Peanuts for the price of one Babyliss. For many barbers, the Peanut is the "daily driver" that handles the majority of the work, while the Babyliss is reserved for the final, ultra-sharp detail.

---

## Who is Each For?

**Buy the Wahl Peanut if:**
- You want a reliable, professional trimmer for under $35.
- You prefer a lightweight tool to reduce hand fatigue.
- You are an at-home user who doesn't need "skeleton style" T-blades.

**Buy the BabylissPro FX if:**
- You are a busy professional barber doing 10+ lineups a day.
- You need the visibility of an exposed T-blade for complex designs or ultra-sharp hairlines.
- You appreciate premium construction and don't mind the $75+ price tag.

---

## Our Verdict

If you have the budget, the **BabylissPro FX** is the better professional trimmer. But for 99% of people, the **Wahl Peanut** is more than enough. It's the smart money choice.
