## Compact Power: Which One Wins?

The Wahl Peanut and the Wahl Stylera often sit next to each other on retail shelves. On paper, they look similar: both are small, both are lightweight, and both claim professional results. 

But once you turn them on, the differences become clear.

---

## Performance: Pivot vs. Rotary

The **Wahl Peanut** uses a pivot motor. This motor type is slower but has more "bite." It handles thick hair lines without bogging down as easily. It's the motor type pros prefer for lining and detailing.

The **Wahl Stylera** typically uses a rotary motor. It's faster and quieter, which feels nice for at-home face trimming. However, it can struggle more with hair bulk or very crisp outlines compared to the Peanut.

---

## Design and Ergonomics

The **Peanut** is shaped like... well, a peanut. This provides two "bulbs" for your hand to grip, making it incredibly maneuverable for flips and scrolls around the ears.

The **Stylera** has a more traditional "wand" shape. It feels modern and sleek, but lacks the "mechanical" grip that the Peanut provides.

---

## Blade System

This is the Peanut's biggest advantage. The **Peanut's blade system** is iconic. Replacement blades are available in every barber supply store on earth. They snap on and off in seconds.

The **Stylera's blades** are harder to find and can be more difficult to replace yourself. 

---

## Who is Each For?

**Buy the Wahl Peanut if:**
- You are a barber or serious at-home fader.
- You want a tool that will last you 5+ years.
- You prioritize lining precision over noise level.

**Buy the Wahl Stylera if:**
- You want a quieter trimmer for basic beard maintenance.
- You prefer the modern "wand" aesthetic.
- You don't mind paying slightly more for cordless convenience by default.

---

## Our Verdict

The **Wahl Peanut remains the king.** It's cheaper, more durable, and has a much better blade replacement ecosystem. The Stylera is a decent modern trimmer, but it doesn't have the "soul" or the raw lining power of the Peanut.
