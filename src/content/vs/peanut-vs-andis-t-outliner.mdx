## The Real Difference: Motor Type

The most important difference between these two trimmers isn't the brand — it's the **motor**.

**Wahl Peanut** uses a pivot motor. Pivot motors are smooth, quiet, and consistent. They handle most hair types well but can struggle slightly with very coarse or thick hair.

**Andis T-Outliner** uses a magnetic motor. Magnetic motors run faster (7,200 SPM vs ~4,800 SPM) and are preferred by professionals doing crisp outlines on coarse or tightly coiled hair. The trade-off: more noise and more vibration.

If you're lining up coarse, textured hair for a client daily — the T-Outliner's motor will feel more confident. If you're doing lighter finishing work or at-home cuts on straight or wavy hair — the Peanut's motor is plenty.

---

## Blade Comparison

The Andis T-blade is a **T-shaped outliner blade** — wider than the Peanut's standard blade, which means you can cover more area quickly. The T-blade can also be zero-gapped (blades adjusted to be flush) for ultra-crisp hairline definition.

The Wahl Peanut blade is narrower and shaped more like a standard clipper blade. It's precise, but you can't zero-gap it without modification.

> **Barber verdict:** If crisp, razor-sharp hairlines are your job every day, the T-Outliner's blade wins. For blending, fading, and general trim work, the Peanut blade is excellent.

---

## Heat Management

This is where the Peanut clearly wins. Andis T-Outliners are notorious for running hot — a known trade-off for the magnetic motor. Many barbers rotate between two T-Outliners to let one cool.

The Peanut stays cool enough to work continuously without discomfort or needing to blade-cool.

---

## Price Point

At ~$30 vs ~$60, the price gap is significant. Unless you specifically need the T-Outliner's capabilities for professional coarse-hair outlining, the Peanut delivers 80–90% of the performance at half the cost.

---

## Who Should Buy Each

### Buy the Wahl Peanut if you are:
- An at-home user wanting clean results without a learning curve
- A barber doing blend/fade work where crisp T-blade outlines aren't the main service
- Working on straight, wavy, or slightly textured hair
- On a budget and want best value

### Buy the Andis T-Outliner if you are:
- A professional barber doing daily outlines on coarse/textured hair
- Comfortable adjusting blade gap and managing heat
- Willing to pay double for professional-grade precision
- Already have a Peanut or other clipper and need a dedicated outliner

---

## Our Verdict

For **professional barbers** who outline daily on coarse hair: the T-Outliner is the industry standard for a reason. Get it.

For **at-home users and barbers who fade more than outline**: the Wahl Peanut is the smarter buy. Half the price, cooler running, cordless option available, and more than capable.
