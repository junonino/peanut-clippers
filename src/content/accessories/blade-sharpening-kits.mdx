## To Sharpen or to Replace?

This is the most common question we get about Peanut blades. 
- **A new replacement blade costs ~$15.**
- **A basic sharpening kit costs ~$25.**

If you are a professional barber with 5 different clippers, sharpening kits pay for themselves in a single month. If you are a home user who only has a single Peanut, it's almost always smarter to just buy a new blade every year.

---

## What's in a Sharpening Kit?

A typical "at-home" clipper sharpening kit includes:
1. **Sharpening Plate:** Usually a glass or flat metal surface.
2. **Sharpening Grit:** A fine powder or paste.
3. **Magnetic Holder:** To hold the tiny Peanut blade while you rub it against the plate.
4. **Brush and Oil:** For final cleanup.

## The Verdict

**For Barbers:** Yes, buy a kit. You'll save hundreds of dollars over the lifetime of your career.

**For Home Users:** No. The risk of misaligning the blade or over-sharpening it is too high. Stick to [buying new replacement blades](/accessories/replacement-blades).
