## How Often Should You Replace?

| Use frequency | Replace every |
|---|---|
| 1–2 cuts/week (at-home) | 12–18 months |
| 5–10 clients/day (barber) | 3–6 months |
| 10+ clients/day (heavy use) | 4–8 weeks |

**Pro barber tip:** Keep a spare blade in your kit. Switching to a fresh blade mid-session when the first one starts pulling is faster than stopping to troubleshoot.

---

## Signs Your Blade Needs Replacing

- Hair pulls or snags during cutting
- Uneven lines that used to be clean
- Visible nicks or chips in the blade teeth
- Increased vibration or unusual sounds
- The blade no longer responds smoothly to the taper lever

If you're experiencing any of these and your blade is clean and oiled, it's time for a replacement.

---

## What About Zero-Gap Blades?

Some aftermarket blades advertise "zero-gap" compatibility with the Wahl Peanut. These are blades adjusted so the cutting blade sits flush with the stationary blade, giving the sharpest possible outline.

**Our take:** Zero-gapping a Peanut is possible but not recommended for beginners. It increases the risk of skin irritation and requires precise alignment. If you need zero-gap sharpness for outlining, consider pairing the Peanut with an Andis T-Outliner — [see our comparison here](/vs/peanut-vs-andis-t-outliner).
