## Guard Size Guide

Understanding guard numbers makes cutting dramatically easier:

| Guard # | Length | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| None (bare blade) | ~0.5mm | Outlines, hairlines, zero-fade |
| #½ | 1.5mm | Super tight fade, skin fade entry |
| #1 | 3mm | Tight military cut, fade start |
| #1.5 | 4.5mm | Mid fade blend |
| #2 | 6mm | Standard short cut, fade blend |
| #3 | 10mm | Longer top taper |
| #4 | 13mm | Medium length all over |

**For a basic home fade kit**, #1 through #3 covers 90% of situations. The taper lever on the Peanut also gives you "in between" lengths without swapping guards.

---

## Barber vs. At-Home Guard Needs

**Barbers** typically use the Peanut without guards — it's primarily a detail and outline trimmer. If you're using it with guards for blending, a #1 and #2 are usually sufficient as the Peanut is rarely the primary bulk-removal tool.

**At-home users** benefit from a full set. The Peanut becomes a surprisingly capable all-in-one trimmer for self-cuts when you have the right guards.

---

## Guard Maintenance

Plastic guards can warp or crack over time, especially if stored in high-heat environments (like a car in summer).

- Rinse guards after every use
- Let them air dry completely before storing
- Replace if cracked — warped guards give uneven cuts

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do full-size Wahl guards fit the Peanut?**
No. The Peanut's blade is narrower than a standard Wahl clipper. Full-size guards won't seat properly and you'll get an uneven cut.

**Can I buy individual guards instead of a set?**
Yes, individual Peanut guards are available on Amazon, but the set is usually cheaper overall.

**My guard won't clip on tight — what's wrong?**
The blade screws may be slightly loose, causing the blade to shift. Snug them up slightly and try again.
