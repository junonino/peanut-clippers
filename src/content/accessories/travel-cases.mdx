## Why You Need a Case

The Wahl Peanut is tiny and portable, which makes it tempting to just throw in a gym bag or a suitcase. **Don't do it.** 

The blade teeth are precision-ground and can chip if they rattle against other metal items (like keys or chargers). More importantly, if the Peanut falls out and lands on its 'nose' (the blade), you'll be spending $15 on a replacement blade immediately.

## Other Considerations

**1. Soft vs. Hard Shell:** We always recommend hard shell (EVA) for clippers. Soft pouches provide zero protection against "crush" forces in a packed bag.

**2. Cord Management:** If you have the corded Peanut, make sure the case is at least 6 inches long so you don't have to "tightly" wrap the cord, which can cause internal wiring breaks over time.
