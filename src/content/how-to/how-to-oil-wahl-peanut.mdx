## Why Oil is Mandatory

Steel blades rubbing against each other at thousands of strokes per minute create immense friction. Without oil, this friction causes:
1. **Heat:** The blade becomes too hot to touch your skin.
2. **Wear:** The precision edges round off, making the blade "dull."
3. **Rust:** Moisture from hair and the air can cause microscopic pitting on the steel.

---

## The 5-Drop Method

Wahl recommends a specific pattern for oiling to ensure total coverage of the cutting surfaces.

1. **Clean the blade first:** Never oil a dirty blade. Use a brush to remove all hair.
2. **Turn the trimmer ON:** This creates a 'distributing' motion as you apply the oil.
3. **Apply 3 drops across the teeth:** One on the left edge, one in the middle, and one on the right edge.
4. **Apply 1 drop to each rail:** These are the side surfaces where the top blade slides against the bottom blade.
5. **Let it run for 10 seconds:** Let the motor distribute the oil into every groove.
6. **Wipe away the excess:** Use a clean paper towel. Excess oil will attract more hair and create a 'sludge.'

---

## When to Oil?

- **Barbers:** After every single client (or every spray of disinfectant).
- **At-Home Users:** Before and after every haircut.

---

## What Oil to Use?

**Only use specialized clipper oil.** 
- Do NOT use WD-40 (it's a solvent, not a long-term lubricant).
- Do NOT use cooking oil (it will go rancid and sticky).
- Do NOT use motor oil (too thick and smells terrible).

Wahl's own blade oil is very inexpensive and one bottle will likely last a home user for years.
