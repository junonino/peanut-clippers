## The Modern Alternative

For years, the "Peanut" meant white plastic. But as barbershop designs moved toward industrial and dark-mode aesthetics, Wahl introduced the Black Peanut. 

It is functionally identical to the [Classic White Peanut](/reviews/wahl-peanut-classic-corded), but the matte-adjacent black finish gives it a completely different vibe on the tool tray.

---

## Why Choose Black Over White?

**1. Cleanliness perception:** On a white clipper, every tiny hair fragment is visible against the body. On the Black Peanut, hair dust and debris are much less obvious, making your station look cleaner during a busy shift.

**2. Modern Vibes:** If your shop has black chairs, dark wood, or metal accents, the black model just fits better.

**3. Grip Texture:** Some users report that the black plastic feels slightly more "tackier" (better grip) than the smooth white plastic, though the difference is minimal.

---

## Maintenance Tip

Because it's black, you need to be **more diligent about cleaning.** Hair buildup inside the blade assembly is harder to see against the dark plastic. Make sure you're using a flashlight or bright station light when you do your weekly deep clean.
