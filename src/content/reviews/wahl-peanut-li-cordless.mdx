## The Case for Going Cordless

Cords are fine until they're not. Wrapping around a client's shoulders, yanking the clipper mid-line, or simply running out of reach — the corded Peanut's only real weakness is its leash.

The Wahl Peanut Li (Lithium Ion) solves exactly that. It's the same compact trimmer, same precision blade, same taper lever — just without the cable. For mobile barbers and at-home users, this changes the experience significantly.

---

## Battery Life: Real-World Testing

Wahl rates the Peanut Cordless at roughly **60 minutes of runtime** per charge. In our testing:

- **Light home use (1–2 cuts/week):** Charges last 10–14 days comfortably
- **Professional use (multiple clients/day):** Expect to charge every 1–2 days
- **Full drain to full charge:** Approximately 70 minutes via the included charger

One thing to note: **lithium ion cells deliver slightly less torque toward the end of a charge cycle.** Most users won't feel this in the cut, but if you're doing very thick or coarse hair, you may notice the blade slowing in the final 10 minutes of battery life.

**Tip:** Keep it on the charger between sessions so it's always at 100%.

---

## Corded Option When You Need It

One underrated feature: the Peanut Li can run plugged in. So if you're mid-cut and it dies, just plug it in and keep going. This hybrid flexibility removes the only real risk of going cordless.

---

## Performance vs. Corded Model

Honestly? **Very similar.** The blade is identical, line-up precision is identical, and the taper lever feels the same. If someone handed you both mid-session without the cord giving it away, you might not notice the difference.

Where the cordless model pulls ahead: **total user experience.** Moving freely around a client, repositioning without thought, doing a bathroom fade without a cord stretched across the sink — these are quality-of-life improvements that matter daily.

## Verdict

The Wahl Peanut Cordless Li is a **worthy upgrade** if the cord has ever been a friction point for you. The performance is on par with the corded model, the battery life is practical for most use cases, and the hybrid corded/cordless operation removes the downside.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Half a point off for battery fade during long sessions and the significant price jump from the corded. But for the cordless convenience, it earns it.
