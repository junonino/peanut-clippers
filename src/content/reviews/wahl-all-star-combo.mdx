## The Power Couple of Barbering

Why buy just a trimmer when you can get the whole station setup? The Wahl All Star Combo (Model 8331) is a legendary pairing that has been the backbone of barber school kits for years. 

It includes:
1. **The Wahl Peanut:** The classic white corded version.
2. **The Wahl Senior/Sterling 4 Clipper:** A high-powered, electromagnetic motor clipper.

---

## The Value Proposition

Buying a Wahl Peanut separately usually costs ~$30. Buying a professional Wahl clipper like the Senior or Designer usually costs ~$70-90. 

By getting the All Star Combo at **$79.99**, you are effectively getting the Peanut for half price or less. It's the most cost-effective way to get professional-grade gear.

---

## Tool 1: The Peanut (The Finisher)

Included in the box is the standard [Wahl Peanut Classic](/reviews/wahl-peanut-classic-corded). You already know why we love it: it's light, it's sharp, and it gets into those tight spots around the ears and neck that larger clippers simply can't reach.

---

## Tool 2: The Clipper (The Workhorse)

The clipper included is a full-size professional Wahl clipper. It's heavy, powerful, and designed to mow through bulk hair all day. It uses standard Wahl guards (included in the set) and features the adjustable taper lever for fading.

**Note:** Unlike the Peanut, which stays relatively cool, the electromagnetic motor in the clipper can get warm after about 45 minutes of continuous use.

---

## Who Is This For?

- **Barber Students:** This is the unofficial 'starter pack.'
- **At-Home 'Self-Cut' Enthusiasts:** If you want to do full haircuts at home, you need both bulk removal and detail trimming. This kit gives you both.
- **Back-up Kit:** Pros often keep this combo in their drawer as a reliable fallback if their cordless tools die.
